# KNet Companion — Brand Guidelines

This kit contains LogoLoom-generated raster and social exports of the standalone connected-K symbol. Canonical production SVGs and outlined wordmarks live in `../svg/`; editable masters live in `../source/`.

## Identity

- The open K identifies the KNet family.
- The linked ring represents the paired companion device.
- The transparent mark is the identity; it does not include a square, rounded rectangle, background, shadow, phone, monitor, QR code, shield, or VPN glyph.
- Platform launchers must supply their own background and mask.

## Exports

- `icon-16.png` through `icon-1024.png`: transparent raster symbols for size-specific use.
- `icon-512.webp`: web-optimized symbol.
- `icon-mono-black.svg` and `icon-mono-white.svg`: single-color symbols.
- `favicon.ico`: browser favicon.
- `og-image.png`, `github-social-preview.png`, and `twitter-header.png`: generated social canvases.
- `logo-full-*`: LogoLoom compatibility aliases containing the symbol. Use the canonical wordmarks from `../svg/` when text is required.

## Colors

- Gradient start: `#4F9CFE`
- Primary: `#3081FC`
- Gradient end: `#2563EB`
- Supporting indigo: `#6366F1`
- Dark surface: `#0D1117`
- Light surface: `#FFFFFF`

## Usage

- Maintain the original aspect ratio.
- Keep clear space of at least 20% of the symbol width.
- Do not stretch, rotate, skew, crop, recolor individual strokes, or add effects.
- Use the monochrome variants where gradients are not supported.
- Check the 16 px asset in context; prefer 24 px or larger for interactive UI.
